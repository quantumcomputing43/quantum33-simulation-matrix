## Evidence and cost checks

### Candidate A — one workflow improvement for a narrow business niche
- **Evidence signal:** a recent report on a New York Fed survey says 46% of surveyed U.S. small and medium businesses use AI; among AI users, only 51% report full or partial integration into daily operations, and 31% report actual sales increases. This supports a possible implementation gap, not guaranteed willingness to buy our service. Secondary report: https://www.techradar.com/pro/small-businesses-that-adopt-ai-are-more-optimistic-about-growth-revenue-and-employment
- **Offer hypothesis:** map one repetitive admin task and deliver a simple template/workflow with human review. Do not promise revenue gains.
- **Upfront cost:** potentially 0 MAD if delivered manually with existing free tools; internet/device/time are excluded from cash spend but are real costs.
- **Risks:** crowded category; buyer pain and willingness to pay unproven; no paid API or subscription allowed.
- **Test:** build one example from public information and present a narrowly scoped paid pilot to a small, relevant set of prospects only after per-message approval.
- **Falsifier:** no relevant buyer acknowledges the problem or none accepts a clearly scoped paid pilot after a small, properly targeted test.

### Candidate B — Google Business Profile / contact-path audit
- **Evidence/cost:** Google says Business Profile management on Search and Maps is available at no charge: https://support.google.com/business/answer/7039811?hl=en-GB
- **Offer hypothesis:** deliver a short audit of public business details, hours, contact path, service descriptions, and review-response gaps; implementation only with owner authorization.
- **Upfront cost:** 0 MAD for manual research; no paid rank-tracking tools.
- **Risks:** strong competition and unclear willingness to pay for a report alone; no ranking or sales guarantees.
- **Test:** prepare one evidence-linked sample report and offer a fixed-scope implementation/pilot.
- **Falsifier:** sample reveals no meaningful issue or target buyers see no value at a paid price.

### Candidate C — repurpose existing client content
- **Offer hypothesis:** transform one client-owned video/article into a small, defined set of captions, short scripts, and post drafts.
- **Upfront cost:** potentially 0 MAD using client-provided assets and free tools.
- **Risks:** crowded market, quality/revision burden, copyright and permission requirements.
- **Test:** make one consented or clearly labeled demonstration using owned/licensed material; offer a fixed deliverable, not vague ongoing content.
- **Falsifier:** production time makes contribution poor, or buyers won't pay for a small trial.
