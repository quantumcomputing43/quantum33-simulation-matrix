## Deal controls
Before sending:
- Verify contact details using the business's current official page or a quick direct confirmation.
- Review the business's current public digital presence and add one real, source-linked observation if available. Do not invent a gap.
- Personalize the opening; send only one relevant message per business.
- Do not attach a mockup or use their logo/photos without permission.
- Do not begin unpaid production; agree scope, price, deadline, revisions, hosting and asset rights first.
- Respect no-contact requests and record silence/refusal without interpreting it as proof of no demand.

## Tracking sheet
For each business, record: date verified; contact channel; specific observation + URL; message sent date; reply; qualified conversation; requested scope; written quote; agreement; payment; delivery time; direct costs.

## Frozen test rule
Start the seven-day validation window when the first valid outreach is sent. Primary endpoint: at least one real payment from an unrelated customer for the defined pilot in that window. If no payment occurs, report the endpoint as not met for this test. Do not call the market validated based on directory listings, simulations, or replies alone.
