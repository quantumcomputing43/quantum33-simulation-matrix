# Research protocol — evidence first

## Questions to answer for each company
1. What does it sell, to whom, and in which geography/languages?
2. What public channels does a customer use to understand services and contact the business?
3. What exact friction can be observed on those channels?
4. What evidence supports that observation, and when was it checked?
5. What impact might it have? Label this as a hypothesis until the company confirms it.
6. Is there a small, low-cost fix within the frozen offer?
7. Is there a public, current business contact route? Is a professional decision-maker publicly identified?

## Evidence labels
- `OBSERVED_FACT`: directly visible in a source; include URL and check date.
- `INFERENCE`: plausible interpretation of an observed fact; list supporting evidence.
- `UNKNOWN`: not established; never fill with a guess.
- `COMPANY_CONFIRMED`: the company explicitly confirms the need during discovery.

## Example
Good: “The public service page lists excursions but provides no visible inquiry action on the page checked on YYYY-MM-DD” plus the URL and a screenshot/note.
Not good: “The company is losing customers” without analytics or company confirmation.
A missing website result in a search engine is not sufficient evidence that a company has no website.

## Decision gate
- `HOLD_RESEARCH`: outdated contact, company status uncertain, or no sourced issue.
- `READY_FOR_HUMAN_REVIEW`: current business contact verified, specific observable issue documented with URL/date, plausible in-scope fix, and no required paid dependency.
- `NOT_A_FIT`: the identified need requires paid integrations, regulated services, complex booking/payment systems, or work outside capability/budget.
- A ready score is not proof of demand or willingness to pay.

## Contact and privacy
Use public business contact details and professional contact channels only. Do not collect private phone numbers, home addresses, sensitive data, or personal information unrelated to business. Use named decision-makers only where their professional role is publicly stated. No impersonation, deception, bulk messaging, or automated sending. Respect opt-outs and do not continue unwanted follow-up.

## Required before first outreach
Human reviewer confirms company status, contact, issue evidence, message accuracy, and offer fit. The seven-day commercial test starts only when a valid message is actually sent.
